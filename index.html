Project Overview

Introduction

This proposal outlines the design and development of a professional travel website and interactive inquiry platform for Homeyage Privé.

The platform will establish a strong digital presence for the company, present its travel and luxury voyage services clearly, and give customers a smooth way to explore services, select package interests, and request customized travel experiences.

Instead of being only a static informative website, the project will include interactive sections that guide visitors through package choices and customer inquiry fields, then generate a structured WhatsApp message for direct communication.

The platform will remain scalable for future online booking, credit card payments, and advanced reservation management.
