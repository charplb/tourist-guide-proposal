Project Overview

Introduction

Thank you for the opportunity to submit this proposal for the development of a professional informative website for Homeyage Privé.

The proposed website will establish a strong online presence for the company, present its travel and luxury voyage services clearly, and allow visitors to submit inquiries directly through WhatsApp using predefined service-based messages.

The website will remain scalable and can later be expanded with advanced online booking or payment features if requested.
