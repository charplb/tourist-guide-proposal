Project Overview

Introduction

Thank you for the opportunity to submit this proposal for the development of a professional digital platform tailored to your business.

The proposed website will establish a strong online presence that showcases your expertise as a Licensed Tourist Guide in Lebanon while presenting your Educational Consulting and Personal Coaching services in a professional and engaging manner.

The platform will allow visitors to explore available services, learn more about your experience, submit inquiries, and communicate directly through WhatsApp for reservations and consultations.

The website is designed with scalability in mind, allowing future integration of online booking and payment solutions.
